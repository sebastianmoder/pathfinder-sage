# Further Resources on Prompting

*Last reviewed: 13 September 2026*

Prompt guidance changes as models and products change. Prefer current,
first-party documentation for a specific product and independent research for
claims that should generalise across systems. Test any technique on the real
task rather than assuming a published pattern will transfer.

## Current product guidance

- [OpenAI model guidance and prompting best practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latest-model)
- [Anthropic prompt library](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/prompt-library/library)

Product guidance can explain supported features and recommended syntax. It is
not independent evidence that using the product improves teaching or learning.

## Research overviews

- Lin, Z. (2024). **How to write effective prompts for large language models**. *Nature Human Behaviour, 8*, 611–615. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-024-01847-2
- Schulhoff, S., Ilie, M., Balepur, N., et al. (2024). **The prompt report: A systematic survey of prompting techniques**. *arXiv*. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.06608

## Open learning resources

- [Prompt Engineering Guide](https://www.promptingguide.ai/)
- [Learn Prompting](https://learnprompting.org/docs)

Check when a resource was last updated, which models it tested, and whether its
examples measure output appearance or actual task accuracy.
