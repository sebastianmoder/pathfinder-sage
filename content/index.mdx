# Practical Guidance for Teaching with AI

## What is SAGE

**Strategies for AI-Guided Education**

SAGE (Strategies for AI-Guided Education) helps higher education educators make
practical, responsible decisions about generative AI. It is written for people
who need to design a course, activity, assessment, or policy—not become AI
specialists.

<Callout type="info">
  Treat AI output as a draft or input to professional judgment, not as an
  authority. Educators remain responsible for educational purpose, accuracy,
  student data, equitable access, and consequential decisions.
</Callout>

## A useful order for new users

1. Use the short [task checklist](/genai/when_to_use).
2. State what students should learn before choosing a tool.
3. Set and communicate the AI-use rules.
4. Test the activity with representative examples.
5. Review the evidence, student experience, accessibility, and unintended effects.

SAGE is an evolving resource. Pages that depend on changing evidence, policy, or
technology include a review date and should always be read alongside local
institutional guidance.
