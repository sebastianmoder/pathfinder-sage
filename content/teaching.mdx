# Better Teaching with AI

This section helps educators use AI in course design and teaching preparation
while keeping learning outcomes, professional judgment, and student rights in
view. Start by deciding what role AI may play in the course or assignment, then
move to the relevant workflow.

- [Setting course and assignment AI rules](/teaching/course_ai_policy/) provides a four-level policy model, disclosure template, and assignment checklist.
- [Designing a curriculum](/teaching/curriculum_design/) explores how AI can support the development of a class curriculum, with learning objectives and topic sequencing.
- [Planning lessons](/teaching/lesson_planning/) demonstrates how to use AI to draft a lesson plan and course activities in alignment with learning goals.
- [Creating course materials](/teaching/course_materials/) shows how AI can help generate engaging case studies, slides, or reading lists.
- [Creating exam questions](/teaching/exam_questions/) covers how AI can assist in crafting diverse, level-appropriate assessments.
- [Creating rubrics](/teaching/rubrics/) discusses how AI can help develop clear, consistent grading rubrics to evaluate student work.
- [Providing feedback](/teaching/feedback/) offers a privacy-aware workflow for drafting evidence-linked feedback while keeping assessment decisions with the educator.

<Callout type="warning">
  Do not enter identifiable student work, confidential assessment materials, or
  other protected information into an unapproved service. See [Responsible AI
  use](/genai/responsible_use) for the full decision process.
</Callout>
